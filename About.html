Who We Are

The International Rescue Committee (IRC) is a humanitarian organisation that responds to the world's most severe humanitarian crises and helps people whose lives have been affected by conflict and disaster.

The organisation works to help people survive, recover and rebuild their lives through programmes in areas such as health, education, protection and economic recovery.

Our Mission

To help people affected by humanitarian crises to survive, recover and gain control of their future.

Our Main Areas of Work

Health

Improving access to essential healthcare and health education.

Education

Supporting learning and educational opportunities for children.

Protection and Assistance

Helping people access support, safety and essential services.
